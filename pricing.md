## Rules

- Hourly billing, 4-hour minimum (5 hours on the 56-Passenger Motorcoach).
- Free hours: book 7+ hours and get 1 free, 12+ hours gets 2 free, 15+ hours gets 3 free.
- Book both transfer legs together and the return leg is 20% off.
- No fees — taxes only.
- A 50% deposit confirms the booking.
- The balance is charged automatically 48 hours before pickup.
